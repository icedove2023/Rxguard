          💡 Tips for Best Results
          


            

              📸
              

                
Good lighting

                

                  Photograph in bright, even light. Avoid shadows across the text.
                

              

            


            

              📐
              

                
Flat and aligned

                

                  Place the prescription flat. Align the camera directly above it.
                

              

            


            

              🔍
              

                
Full prescription visible

                

                  Capture the entire document including the prescriber's stamp and date.
                

              

            


            

              📄
              

                
Use PDF when possible

                

                  Electronic/printed prescriptions in PDF format give the highest OCR accuracy.
